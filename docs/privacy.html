Trackd Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Trackd ("Trackd", "we", "us") is a job-application tracker for iOS, Android, and web. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's used.

In short: we store the job-application data you enter, and — if you choose to forward recruiter emails to your personal Trackd inbox — the content of those emails, so we can automatically move your applications through your pipeline. We do not sell your data.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, and authentication credentials, provided when you sign up.
  • Application data — company names, roles, statuses, salary ranges, notes, and dates you enter into your pipeline.
  • Forwarded email content — if you forward or set up filters to send recruiter emails to your personal @agentmail.to (or custom domain) Trackd inbox, we receive and store the sender address, subject line, and a preview of the body so it can be classified and matched to an application.
  • Push notification tokens — a device-specific token used to deliver notifications (e.g., "Stripe moved to Interview").
  • Usage & device information — basic technical data (platform, app version) needed to operate and debug the service.

How we use your information

  • To operate your application pipeline, kanban board, and analytics.
  • To classify forwarded recruiter emails using an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) and automatically update the matching application's status.
  • To send you push notifications about status changes, interviews, offers, and "ghosted" applications.
  • To enforce plan limits (e.g., the free-tier active-application cap) and prevent abuse.
  • To maintain and improve the reliability and security of the service.

Third parties we share data with

We use the following service providers ("subprocessors") to operate Trackd. Each only receives the data necessary to perform its function:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting.
  • AgentMail — provisions and receives mail for your personal Trackd inbox.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — classifies forwarded email content (sender, subject, body preview) to detect status changes. Email content sent for classification is not used by Anthropic to train models under their API terms.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data retention & deletion

We retain your account and application data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual applications at any time in the app. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below — we will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Security

Application data is protected with row-level security so that only you can read or write your own records. Data is encrypted in transit. Webhook requests from our email provider are cryptographically signature-verified before processing.

Children's privacy

Trackd is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
